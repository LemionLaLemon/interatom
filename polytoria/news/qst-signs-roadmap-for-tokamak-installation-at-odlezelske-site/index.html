QST Signs Roadmap for Tokamak Installation at Odlezelské Site

The company outlines its plans for the installation of a new tokamak at the Odlezelské site.

March 28, 2026

News > QST Signs Roadmap for Tokamak Installation at Odlezelské Site

On 28 March, 2026, QST signed a roadmap for the installation of a new tokamak at the Odlezelské site. This strategic plan outlines the key milestones and objectives for the project, ensuring a structured and efficient approach to the installation process.

Many contractors and engineers will be involved in the installation process, working diligently to meet the planned milestones. With the support of the project management team, the installation is expected to progress smoothly and achieve its objectives within the scheduled timeframe.

Currently, preparations are underway to ensure all necessary components and infrastructure are in place for the tokamak installation. This includes site assessments, equipment procurement, and coordination with various stakeholders to ensure a seamless installation process.

During the installation phase, safety and quality control measures will be strictly enforced to ensure the successful completion of the project. Technicians and engineers will monitor the progress closely, providing real-time updates and ensuring adherence to the highest standards of safety and efficiency.
