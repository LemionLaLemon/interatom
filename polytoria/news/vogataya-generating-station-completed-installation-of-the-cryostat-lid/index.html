Vogataya Generating Station Completed Installation of the Cryostat Lid

The installation of the cryostat lid marks a significant milestone in the station's operational capabilities.

April 22, 2026

News > Vogataya Generating Station Completed Installation of the Cryostat Lid

On 22 April, 2026, Vogataya Generating Station successfully completed the installation of the cryostat lid, a critical component in the station's nuclear reactor system. This achievement represents a major milestone in the station's operational capabilities and underscores the commitment to maintaining the highest standards of safety and efficiency.

With the help from CIEMAT, the assembled cryostat lid was carefully positioned and secured, ensuring optimal performance and safety for the reactor. The cryostat lid serves as a crucial barrier, maintaining the integrity of the reactor's internal environment and supporting the overall efficiency of the power generation process.
