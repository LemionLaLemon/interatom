Interatom Power's First Certified Reactor Operator

Celebrating a Milestone in Nuclear Safety and Operational Excellence

March 28, 2026

News > Interatom Power's First Certified Reactor Operator

On 28 March, 2026, Interatom Power celebrated the achievement of its first certified reactor operator. This milestone marks a significant step in the company's commitment to safety, training, and operational excellence in the field of nuclear energy.

The certified reactor operator has undergone extensive training and evaluation to ensure proficiency in managing reactor operations. This certification demonstrates the individual's capability to handle complex scenarios and maintain the highest standards of safety and efficiency.

Interatom Power continues to invest in the development of its workforce, providing ongoing training and professional growth opportunities. The certification of the first reactor operator is a testament to the company's dedication to excellence and its proactive approach to safety and operational standards.

As the company advances its nuclear energy initiatives, the presence of certified reactor operators will play a crucial role in ensuring the safe and efficient operation of its facilities. This achievement underscores Interatom Power's commitment to maintaining the highest levels of expertise and reliability in the industry.
