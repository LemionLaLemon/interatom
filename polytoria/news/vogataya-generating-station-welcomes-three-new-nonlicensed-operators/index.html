Vogataya Generating Station Welcomes Three New Nonlicensed Operators

The operators will aid control room operations and enhance plant safety.

April 19, 2026

News > Vogataya Generating Station Welcomes Three New Nonlicensed Operators

Vogataya Generating Station, a leading nuclear power facility, is proud to announce the addition of three new nonlicensed operators to its team. These operators will play a crucial role in supporting control room operations and ensuring the continued safety and efficiency of the plant.

The new nonlicensed operators have undergone rigorous training and have demonstrated exceptional skills in various aspects of plant operations. They will assist licensed operators in monitoring and controlling the plant's systems, contributing to the overall safety and reliability of the facility.

InterATOM Power is committed to maintaining the highest standards of safety and operational excellence at Vogataya Generating Station. The addition of these skilled nonlicensed operators is a testament to our dedication to continuous improvement and our commitment to providing clean, reliable energy to our customers.

We welcome our new team members and look forward to their contributions to the success of Vogataya Generating Station.

It is truly a huge step forward for the operational safety of Vogataya Generating Station to have three fully qualified nonlicensed operators added to our control room crew. We hope the operators will be able to train new staff in the future so we can keep up our commitment to safety, and to continue to operator the plant adhering to written procedures safely and efficiently. stated Lemion, Operations Director of Vogataya Generating Station, Odlezelské Site.
