Sawtooth Energy Center Shuts Down for a Refueling Outage

The station temporarily halts operations to perform essential maintenance and refueling activities.

April 5, 2026

News > Sawtooth Energy Center Shuts Down for a Refueling Outage

On 5 April, 2026, Sawtooth Energy Center temporarily ceased operations to undergo a scheduled refueling outage. This planned shutdown allows for the replacement of spent fuel and the performance of critical maintenance tasks, ensuring the continued safe and efficient operation of the reactor.

Many contractors were involved in the refueling process, working diligently to complete the necessary tasks within the planned timeframe. With the help from plant operators, the refueling outage is in progress and is scheduled to finish and return to nominal operations on 8 May, 2026.

Currently, operations are in the process of shuffling old fuel from the center of the core to the periphery, ensuring a balanced and efficient refueling process. Later on, new fuel will be loaded into the core to resume normal operations, ensuring sufficient fuel supply for continued energy production.

During a refueling outage, maintenance in areas normally inaccessible is conducted to ensure the continued safe and efficient operation of the reactor. Technicians monitor the condition of the fuel and other critical components via In-Core Cameras, which provide real-time data to support decision-making and ensure the highest standards of safety. 2000 independent contractors were brought on site to assist with various tasks, highlighting the scale and complexity of the operation.
